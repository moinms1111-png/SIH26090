The problem

Great craft should not require great digital literacy.

The solution starts from the realities faced by artisans: language barriers, product presentation challenges, connectivity limitations, and the effort required to create digital marketplace listings.

01
⌨️

Typing Barrier

Creating descriptions and digital listings can be difficult for users with limited digital literacy.

02
📷

Presentation Barrier

Products photographed in homes or workshops may need enhancement before being presented digitally.

03
🌐

Connectivity & Market Barrier

Low-bandwidth environments and marketplace processes can create additional friction for small sellers.

The solution

The artisan does two things. AI handles the rest.

The core interaction is intentionally simple: provide a photo and speak naturally in a supported language.

Feasibility

Designed for real-world constraints — and a focused 21-day MVP sprint.

The development roadmap follows the three-week structure already planned for the project: app UI and multilingual mode, backend and dashboard, then marketplace integration and rollout readiness.

Challenge → Response

📶 Poor internet access

Offline storage with deferred uploads when connectivity returns.

🗣 Dialect accuracy

Audio read-back lets the artisan verify the generated content.

👥 Onboarding

Cluster-based setup can involve SHG leaders and community support personnel.

💰 Pricing uncertainty

AI suggestions remain editable through manual price overrides.

Why the approach can scale

Android-first scope

Keeps initial development targeted.

Open-source AI core

Reduces dependence on per-call AI licensing for core models.

Low infrastructure footprint

Designed around lightweight client communication.

Cluster-based distribution

Community networks can support adoption across artisan clusters.
